# Sonora

The palette sampling algorithm in `src/Helpers/CoverPalette.cs` is adapted to C#
from Sonora `crates/ui/src/palette.rs`, revision
`0e9624ee48e274508c615d7f40a83a8afeac2566`.
The neutral light/dark color tokens and the UI treatments are based on Sonora's
`crates/ui/src/theme.rs`, shared controls and view implementations at that revision.
