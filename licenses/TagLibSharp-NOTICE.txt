TagLibSharp 2.3.0

chilimusic uses the unmodified TagLibSharp library to read local music metadata,
embedded cover art and lyrics. TagLibSharp.dll is distributed as a separate
assembly and may be replaced with a compatible build.

Project and corresponding source:
https://github.com/mono/taglib-sharp
https://github.com/mono/taglib-sharp/tree/TaglibSharp-2.3.0.0
